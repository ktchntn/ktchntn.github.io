Io privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Io is a private assistant run by Keegan for members of her family. This page explains what happens to Google data when a family member connects their Google account to Io.

What Io accesses

Only if you connect your Google account, and only for your own requests:

  • Gmail (read-only): Io can search and read your messages to answer your questions. It can't send, delete, or change email.
  • Google Calendar: Io can read your events, and can create, change, or delete events when you ask and confirm.

How your data is used

  • Google data is used only to answer your own requests to Io.
  • To answer a request, the relevant email or calendar content is sent to an AI model provider (through OpenRouter) for processing, along with your message.
  • Google data isn't sold, isn't used for advertising, and isn't used to train AI models.
  • Io's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see it

  • Your Google connection belongs to your own Io agent. Other family members' agents can't use it, and Io doesn't share your email or calendar with other family members.
  • Keegan runs the server and has technical access to it.
  • Messages pass through Discord and an AI provider to work.

Storage and security

  • Your Google sign-in token is stored on Keegan's private server, in a file only the Io service can read, and is never shared.
  • Io doesn't keep a separate copy of your mailbox or calendar. Content Io reads to answer you may appear in your own chat history with Io.

Disconnecting

  • You can remove Io's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can also ask Keegan to delete your stored sign-in token and chat history.
