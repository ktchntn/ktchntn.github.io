Io 🌙

A private AI assistant for one family.

Io is a private AI assistant that Keegan runs for her family on her own server. It isn't a public service and isn't available to anyone outside the family.

Family members talk to Io in Discord. If they choose to, each person can connect their own Google account so Io can help with their email and calendar, for example "what's on my calendar this week?" or "do I have any unread email about the trip?"

Each person's Google connection is separate: Io only uses a person's Google account when that same person is the one asking.
